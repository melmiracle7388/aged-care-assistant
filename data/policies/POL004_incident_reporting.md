# POL-004: Incident Reporting
*FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY for a made-up facility. Not for real clinical use.*

## 1. Purpose
To make sure every incident is recorded, reviewed and learned from.

## 2. What counts as an incident
- Falls, near-falls and injuries
- Medication errors or near-misses
- Choking episodes
- Skin tears, bruises or pressure injuries found on a resident
- Behaviour incidents, including resident-to-resident
- Any suspected abuse or neglect
- Equipment failures

## 3. What to do
1. Make sure the resident is safe and tell the RN straight away.
2. Complete the incident report before the end of your shift. Do not leave it for the next shift.
3. Write only the facts: what you saw, what time, what action was taken. Do not guess or blame.
4. The RN reviews the report and notifies the family and doctor where needed.

## 4. Serious incidents
- Suspected abuse, serious injury or a death must be reported to the manager at once.
- The manager decides about notifying the regulator within the required timeframe.

## 5. Agency staff
Agency staff must also complete the incident report and hand over verbally to the RN before leaving.
