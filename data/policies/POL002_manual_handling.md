# POL-002: Manual Handling and Safe Transfers
*FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY for a made-up facility. Not for real clinical use.*

## 1. Purpose
To keep residents and staff safe when moving, lifting and repositioning.

## 2. Before any transfer
- Read the resident's care plan for the transfer method (independent, 1 staff, 2 staff, stand-aid or hoist).
- Never use a different method from the care plan. If you think the resident's ability has changed, stop and tell the RN.
- Check the equipment: brakes on, sling the right size and in good condition, battery charged.
- Explain to the resident what you are about to do.

## 3. Hoist transfers
- A full hoist always needs 2 trained staff.
- Only staff who have completed hoist training may use a hoist. Agency staff must confirm their training with the nurse in charge.
- Use the sling listed in the care plan.

## 4. Stand-aid and transfer belt
- 1 trained staff member may use a stand-aid or transfer belt if the care plan allows.
- Keep your back straight, bend your knees and keep the resident close to you.

## 5. Never
- Never lift a resident manually by yourself.
- Never pull a resident by their arms.
- Never use a hoist or equipment that is damaged. Tag it and report it.

## 6. If unsure
Ask the nurse in charge. Do not guess.
