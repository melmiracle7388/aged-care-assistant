# POL-001: Falls Prevention and Response
*FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY for a made-up facility. Not for real clinical use.*

## 1. Purpose
To reduce falls and make sure every fall is responded to quickly and safely.

## 2. Scope
All care staff, including agency and casual staff.

## 3. Preventing falls
- Check the resident's care plan for their falls risk (Low, Medium or High) at the start of each shift.
- Keep walkers, call bells, glasses and drinks within reach.
- Keep floors dry and clear. Report spills and clutter straight away.
- Residents with a High falls risk need regular checks and staff standby for toileting and walking, as set out in their care plan.
- Make sure beds are at the lowest height and brakes are on when a resident is in bed, unless the care plan says otherwise.

## 4. If a resident has fallen
1. Stay with the resident and call for help. Press the emergency bell.
2. Do NOT move the resident if you suspect an injury, pain in the head, neck, back or hip, or if they cannot move a limb.
3. Reassure the resident and keep them warm and comfortable.
4. The Registered Nurse (RN) in charge assesses the resident before they are moved. Staff may only help the resident up after the RN says it is safe.
5. If the resident has hit their head, or is taking blood-thinning medicine, tell the RN immediately.
6. If there is severe injury, loss of consciousness or the resident is unresponsive, the RN calls 000.

## 5. After a fall
- Complete an incident report before the end of the shift (see POL-004).
- Observations are done as directed by the RN.
- The RN notifies the next of kin and the doctor.
- The falls risk and care plan are reviewed by the RN.
