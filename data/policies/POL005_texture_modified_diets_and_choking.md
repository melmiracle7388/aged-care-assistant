# POL-005: Texture-Modified Diets, Thickened Fluids and Choking
*FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY for a made-up facility. Not for real clinical use.*

## 1. Purpose
To make sure each resident gets the right food and drink texture, and to respond safely to choking.

## 2. Diet and fluid levels (IDDSI)
- Food: Level 7 Regular or Easy to Chew, Level 6 Soft & Bite-Sized, Level 5 Minced & Moist, Level 4 Pureed.
- Fluids: Level 0 Thin, Level 1 Slightly Thick, Level 2 Mildly Thick, Level 3 Moderately Thick.
- The level for each resident is in their care plan and on the dining room list. Do not change it.

## 3. Before serving a meal or drink
- Check the resident's name, texture level and allergies on the care plan or dining list.
- Never give a resident food or drink at a different level from their care plan.
- Thickened fluids must be prepared using the thickener and method on the care plan.
- Sit the resident upright, at 90 degrees, for meals and for 30 minutes afterwards.

## 4. During the meal
- Give the resident time. Do not rush.
- Watch for coughing, a wet or gurgly voice, food held in the mouth, or watery eyes.
- If these signs happen, stop feeding and tell the RN.

## 5. If a resident is choking
1. Call for help immediately and press the emergency bell.
2. If the resident can cough, encourage them to keep coughing.
3. If they cannot cough, speak or breathe, follow your first aid training (back blows and chest or abdominal thrusts as trained).
4. The RN or another staff member calls 000.
5. Complete an incident report afterwards (see POL-004).

## 6. Agency staff
If you do not know a resident's diet level, ask the nurse in charge before offering any food or drink.
