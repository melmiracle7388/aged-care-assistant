# POL-003: Infection Prevention and Control
*FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY for a made-up facility. Not for real clinical use.*

## 1. Purpose
To stop infections from spreading between residents, staff and visitors.

## 2. Hand hygiene
- Clean your hands before and after contact with a resident, before handling food, after toileting care and after removing gloves.
- Use alcohol-based hand rub unless your hands are visibly dirty. If they are, wash with soap and water.

## 3. Personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Wear gloves for contact with body fluids. Change gloves between residents.
- Wear a gown and mask when the care plan or signage requires it, for example during an outbreak or isolation.
- Put PPE on before entering the room and remove it before leaving.

## 4. Staff who are unwell
- Do not come to work if you have vomiting, diarrhoea or fever.
- Staff with symptoms must stay away from work for at least 48 hours after the symptoms stop, or as directed by management.
- Tell your manager or the agency before the shift.

## 5. Outbreaks
- If 2 or more residents have similar symptoms, tell the RN in charge immediately.
- Follow all signage, isolation and visitor instructions given by the RN.

## 6. Cleaning and waste
- Clean shared equipment between residents.
- Put used sharps in the sharps container. Never recap needles.
- Soiled linen goes into the correct bag.
