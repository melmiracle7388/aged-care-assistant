# POL-006: Reporting Changes in a Resident's Condition
*FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY for a made-up facility. Not for real clinical use.*

## 1. Purpose
To make sure care staff pass on any change in a resident quickly to the Registered Nurse (RN).

## 2. Care staff do not diagnose
Care staff notice and report. The RN assesses and decides what happens next.

## 3. Tell the RN straight away if a resident:
- is suddenly more confused, sleepy or agitated than usual
- is eating or drinking much less than normal
- has pain, a new rash, swelling or skin damage
- has vomiting, diarrhoea or a fever
- has trouble breathing or looks pale or sweaty
- has a new cough, or coughs or chokes when eating or drinking
- has not passed urine or had a bowel movement for longer than usual
- has had a fall or a near-fall

## 4. How to report
1. Stay with the resident if they look unwell. Ask another staff member to find the RN.
2. Tell the RN what you saw, when you saw it and what is different from normal.
3. Write it in the progress notes before the end of your shift.

## 5. Emergencies
If the resident is unresponsive, not breathing or in severe distress, press the emergency bell and call for the RN. The RN calls 000.

## 6. Handover
Pass on any changes you noticed to the next shift at handover. Agency staff must hand over to the RN before leaving.
